Founder, Spudy Intelligence Technology

Matipaishe started Spudy Intelligence Technology to give Zimbabwean businesses the same technology advantages larger companies take for granted — a website that converts visitors, an app that actually works, and marketing decisions backed by data instead of guesswork.

Technology and marketing, under one roof.

Most businesses end up juggling a web developer, a social media freelancer, an app agency and a print shop — each with their own timeline and their own price. Spudy Intelligence Technology builds all of it as one connected system, so your website, your marketing and your brand materials actually work together.

We work with small businesses getting online for the first time, and growing companies ready to automate, scale and compete digitally.
